Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Edappadi Kadai is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is treated with highest security, transparency, and integrity.

📅 Effective: August 2026 📍 Salem, Tamil Nadu, India 🛡️ Package: com.edappadikadai.app
📋
1. Data We Collect

To provide fast, reliable fresh meat and vegetable delivery in Tamil Nadu, India, we collect the following personal and operational information:

  • Identity Details: Full Name, Phone Number, and optional Email Address for account creation and login.
  • Delivery Address & GPS Location: Physical delivery address and precise/approximate GPS coordinates (Latitude & Longitude) to route delivery drivers accurately to your doorstep.
  • Order & Cart Details: Selected products, weights/quantities, custom cutting preferences, active cart items, and complete order history.
  • UPI Transaction References: Transaction ID (txnId), approval reference number (approvalRef), and payment status received via UPI Intent callback.
🔒
Zero Financial Credentials Stored: We strictly DO NOT collect, store, or process your Bank Account Numbers, Debit/Credit Card Details, CVV, or UPI PINs.
⚙️
2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly for core app operations and customer convenience:

  • Account Authentication: Secure sign-in and session verification using Firebase Authentication (SMS Phone Auth & Google Sign-In).
  • Order Fulfillment & Delivery: Preparing your custom meat/vegetable cuts and guiding assigned delivery partners to your location.
  • Real-time Push Notifications: Sending instant order status updates (Packed, Out for Delivery, Delivered) via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • Customer Support: Resolving order queries, refunds, delivery assistance, and feedback via phone or chat.
  • Loyalty Points & Rewards: Managing cashback credits, wallet balances, and promotional coupons.
☁️
3. Firebase Services & Cloud Infrastructure

Edappadi Kadai uses industry-standard, secure Google Cloud and Firebase services:

  • Firebase Authentication: Secure phone number OTP & email authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore: Encrypted, real-time cloud database for storing menus, orders, customer profiles, and shop operational status.
  • Cloud Storage: Secure storage for product images and order invoice attachments.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Encrypted push notification delivery to Android devices.
  • Cloud Functions: Serverless backend execution for order validation, SMS notifications, and secure account deletion.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Diagnostic crash logging to identify and resolve stability issues without capturing personal data.
  • Firebase App Check (Play Integrity): Protecting the app against unauthorized clients and tampering.
💳
4. UPI Payments (Intent Flow)

Payments on Edappadi Kadai are processed via direct UPI Intent Flow (upi://pay deep link):

  • When you checkout using UPI, our app initiates a deep link directly opening your installed NPCI-approved UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, CRED, etc.).
  • The entire transaction and UPI PIN authorization occur inside your chosen UPI payment app, governed by your bank's security protocols.
  • Our app only receives the confirmation response (Transaction ID and approval reference) to verify order completion.
🛡️
NPCI Compliance: We never have access to your bank passwords, PINs, or card details.
🔒
5. Data Encryption & Security Standards

We enforce strict technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data:

  • HTTPS Enforced: 100% of network traffic between your Android device and our backend uses TLS/HTTPS encryption with cleartext traffic strictly blocked by Android Network Security Config.
  • Hardware-Backed Local Storage: On-device sensitive caches and session tokens are encrypted with AES-256 GCM using master keys stored securely in the Android Keystore.
  • Cryptographic Hashing: Credentials and verification codes are hashed using SHA-256 and secure cryptographic algorithms.
  • Database Security Rules: Firestore database access is governed by strict server-side rules preventing unauthorized reading or tampering.
🚫
6. Third-Party Data Sharing

Your personal data is never sold, rented, or monetized:

  • We DO NOT integrate third-party advertising networks, ad trackers, or commercial data brokers.
  • Data is shared strictly with Google Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) exclusively to host infrastructure and process essential app functions.
  • Assigned delivery drivers receive only your delivery address and phone number for the active order.
🗑️
7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your profile and order data as long as your account is active to facilitate order history and rewards:

  • You may delete your account at any time via the in-app Delete Account option in Profile Settings or by contacting admin.
  • Account deletion invokes our secure Cloud Function (deleteCustomerAccount), permanently removing your profile, saved addresses, authentication records, and favorites from our database.
⚠️
Account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed once processed.
👶
8. Children's Privacy

Our app and delivery services are not directed to or intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

⚖️
9. Your Rights & Choices

Under applicable Indian data protection laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and IT Act, you have the right to:

  • Access and review personal information associated with your account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate phone numbers, names, or addresses.
  • Request complete deletion of your account and personal records.
  • Opt-out of promotional notifications by adjusting notification preferences.
